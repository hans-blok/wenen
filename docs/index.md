
# Towards an explicit agent ecosystem

Many IT professionals work daily with a patchwork of scripts, tools, plug-ins and manual steps. Much of it has grown over the years: a PowerShell script here, a Python utility there, a few clever settings in a CI pipeline, and some manual work that everyone “just does that way”. The result is an implicit landscape of tooling and ad-hoc automation. It often works fine – until it doesn’t.

In such an environment it is difficult to see exactly which steps are taken when, by whom or what, and based on which assumptions. Processes then depend on undocumented knowledge, individual routines and hidden dependencies. This makes software development fragile: a small change can have unexpected side effects, errors only become visible late, and it is hard to reconstruct after the fact exactly what happened.

This way of working has direct consequences for predictability, visibility and accountability. Predictability suffers because the actual pipeline is not clearly and repeatably described, but is hidden in local scripts and personal practices. Visibility is limited because nobody has a complete, shared picture of the chain; dashboards and logs may show fragments, but not the whole. And accountability becomes diffuse: when something goes wrong, it is difficult to determine which step, tool or decision was the decisive factor.

This also puts governance under pressure. Policy agreements on quality, security and compliance are only sustainable when it is clear which steps are taken in the development and delivery process, and whether those steps meet the agreed standards. In an implicit and fragmented tooling landscape, that assurance is vulnerable. A structural risk of errors, omissions and incidents arises, and improvements often remain one-off or person-dependent rather than durably embedded.

## What we believe

We believe that experienced IT professionals can build better, more reliable software when they work with an agent ecosystem that is explicit, governable and traceable, rather than continuing to work with implicit tooling and ad-hoc automation, because our solution structurally ensures predictability, visibility and accountability.
